Status Effects

Status effects are buffs or debuffs that are applicable to both enemies and players. These effects originate from various sources, but only certain status effects can be stacked. Indicated by their different colored text/icon.

When reapplying an already existing buff/debuff it will refresh the cooldown of the buff/debuff unless stated otherwise
